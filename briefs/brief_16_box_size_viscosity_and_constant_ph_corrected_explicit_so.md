# Research Brief · Box-Size, Viscosity, and Constant-pH Corrected Explicit-Solvent Bimolecular Association and Permeation Suite

- Category · Settle now
- Subfield · Protein Association & Brownian Dynamics

## Problem Statement

Explicit-solvent Weighted Ensemble k_on and membrane permeability P_m studies routinely omit Yeh-Hummer 1/L periodic hydrodynamic self-diffusion corrections, apply temperature-independent water viscosity ratios, or freeze ionizable solute charge states across low-dielectric bilayers, masking force-field errors behind compensating transport artifacts.

## Concrete Attack Plan

Run barnase-barstar association across three cubic box sizes (L = 8, 11, and 15 nm) in TIP3P and OPC water at 283 K, 298 K, and 310 K to verify collapse onto a single box-independent k_on after Yeh-Hummer and Ando Green-Kubo viscosity corrections, and benchmark tacrine and zacopride POPC permeation under constant-pH Weighted Ensemble across varying bulk-water slab thicknesses l_D.
