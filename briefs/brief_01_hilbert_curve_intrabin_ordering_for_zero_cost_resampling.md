# Research Brief · Hilbert curve intrabin ordering for zero cost resampling variance reduction

- Category · Speed up
- Subfield · Foundations & Exact Resampling

## Problem Statement

Within bin cloning and merging in Weighted Ensemble engines typically orders walkers arbitrarily before residual or systematic selection, forfeiting multidimensional spatial negative association proven in Gerber, Chopin and Whiteley (2019).

## Concrete Attack Plan

Sort walkers inside each occupied bin along a d dimensional Hilbert curve over auxiliary collective variables prior to residual stratified selection, then benchmark empirical flux variance scaling against unordered binned multinomial resampling on synthetic Trp cage Markov dynamics.
