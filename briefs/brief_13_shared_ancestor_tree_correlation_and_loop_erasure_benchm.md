# Research Brief · Shared ancestor tree correlation and loop erasure benchmark for LPATH pathway histograms

- Category · Estimator audit
- Subfield · Software, GPU Engines & Workflows

## Problem Statement

Bogetti et al. 2023 clusters condensed state symbol strings traced from WESTPA splitting trees in LPATH, whereas Suarez & Zuckerman 2018 removes diffusive recrossings through shortest path fundamental sequences. Quantifying how shared parent branches in Weighted Ensemble trees and state alphabet resolution perturb inferred route weights will establish calibrated uncertainty intervals for pathway classes.

## Concrete Attack Plan

Generate synthetic branching path ensembles with known ground truth route fractions on the two channel potential of Suarez & Zuckerman 2018 and alanine dipeptide data from Bogetti et al. 2023, comparing condensed string LPATH clusters against fundamental sequences and ancestor block bootstrap intervals.
