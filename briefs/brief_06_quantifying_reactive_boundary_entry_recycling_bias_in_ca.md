# Research Brief · Quantifying Reactive Boundary Entry Recycling Bias in Cavity Unbinding Kinetics

- Category · Settle now
- Subfield · Steady State, Hill Flux & Non Markovian Rates

## Problem Statement

Baudel, Guyader & Lelievre 2023 proved that inverse stationary sink flux reproduces the equilibrium mean reaction time strictly when recycled trajectories sample the reactive boundary entry distribution on boundary A rather than a single crystal pose or Boltzmann basin slice, while inertial Langevin dynamics in Copperman & Zuckerman 2020 fall outside the one dimensional overdamped Smoluchowski monotonicity bounds of Copperman et al. 2019. Recycling unbinding walkers to a single bound pose risks multi decade flux distortion when internal pocket gates relax slowly.

## Concrete Attack Plan

Build two color history labeled equilibrium and source sink ensembles modeled on Suarez & Zuckerman 2014 and Pratt et al. 2019 across inertial Langevin 2D potentials from Copperman & Zuckerman 2020 and host guest dissociation systems from Ray et al. 2020. Measure total variation distance between single pose feedback, Boltzmann basin initiation, and empirical reactive boundary entry distributions, then compare inverse flux MFPT errors against theoretical quasi stationary bias bounds from Baudel, Guyader & Lelievre 2023.
